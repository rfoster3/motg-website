Extra gates for the 100% version:
- Pick ONE number. MOTG-HANDOFF-BRIEF says 10%; the store's shared product template says "100% of proceeds". Every ad, page and listing must say the same thing.
- Write down what "proceeds" means (for example: sale price minus product cost, shipping and payment fees) and put it on the product page or FAQ, so "100%" can be shown to be true.
- The college's written sign-off should name the 100% figure.
- Both 100% videos (launch AND pre-launch) show a close-up of the embroidery made with software, matched to the logo art. Before either runs, check it against a real stitch-out from the supplier, and swap in a photo of the real stitching if it differs.
- Before running outside Tennessee, check whether a "commercial co-venturer" registration or disclosure applies (some states require it when a business advertises that sales benefit a charity or school program). Ask a lawyer or the college's development office.
- Only one giving version may run. If 100% is chosen, retire the 10% stills, videos and copy above.
