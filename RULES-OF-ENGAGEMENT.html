Magicians on the Green

Terms of Engagement with LeMoyne-Owen College

Draft for discussion. Not legal advice. Have a Memphis small-business or sports and education attorney finalize before anyone signs.

MOTG: Magicians on the Green [LLC once formed], an independent supporter brand operated by Dominique Worthen.

The College: LeMoyne-Owen College, including its Athletics and Advancement offices.

1What MOTG is

MOTG is a private supporter and booster brand created by Coach Worthen to promote the LeMoyne-Owen golf program and rally fans behind it. MOTG is a separate business. It is not the College, does not speak for the College, and is not an official College store unless this document says otherwise.

2Use of name and marks

The College gives MOTG permission to reference the "Magicians" name and the golf program on merchandise and marketing, on these terms: non-exclusive, revocable with written notice, subject to the College's reasonable brand review.

MOTG's own original artwork stays owned by MOTG. That includes the "Magicians on the Green" wordmark, the wizard character, and the M logomark as drawn by or for MOTG. If this deal ends, MOTG keeps its own artwork and the College keeps its own marks.

3What the golf team gets

MOTG supports the golf program in two ways:

  • 10 percent of the sale price of every merchandise item, before shipping and tax.
  • A guaranteed minimum of $1,000 per year to the program. If 10 percent of sales totals less than $1,000 in a year, MOTG pays the difference so the team still receives at least $1,000 that year.

Payments go to the program through the College's Advancement office and are reported quarterly. MOTG is a for-profit business and keeps its remaining margin. MOTG will not publicly claim "100 percent goes to the team" for merchandise sales. The honest public line is "10 percent of every purchase supports LeMoyne-Owen golf."

4Donations go straight to the College

Any charitable donation MOTG points people toward goes directly to the College's official giving page, earmarked for golf. MOTG does not collect, hold, or control donated money. This keeps donations tax-deductible for the giver and keeps team funds under the College's control.

5Players (name, image, likeness)

MOTG will not use any student-athlete's name, photo, or likeness to sell or promote anything without a signed release from that player and approval from the College's compliance officer.

6Independence and liability

MOTG runs its own store, pays its own costs, carries its own liability insurance (including for any tournament or event), and handles its own taxes and legal compliance. The College is not responsible for MOTG's operations, and MOTG is not responsible for the College's.

7Coach Worthen's role

Coach Worthen's involvement in MOTG has been disclosed to and approved by the College under its conflict-of-interest and outside-employment policies. Approvals: Athletic Director ________, Advancement ________, Compliance ________.

8Term and exit

This runs for [one year] and renews [automatically] unless either side gives [30 or 60] days written notice.
