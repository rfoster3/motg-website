The Magicians

Our Story

Magicians on the Green is the fundraising home of the LeMoyne-Owen College golf team — a proud HBCU program in Memphis, Tennessee.

Why we exist

More than merch

Building a college golf program takes more than talent. It takes clubs, balls, travel, hotels, and entry fees — the quiet costs that decide who gets to compete. Magicians on the Green turns fans, alumni, and partners into the fuel behind every round.

The golf wizard says it best: a little magic, the right gear, and a community behind you can send a young player farther than they ever imagined.
